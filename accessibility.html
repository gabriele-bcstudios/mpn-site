Accessibility Statement

This statement applies to the MPN — Making People Native website and online tutoring platform, operated by MPN Agency Ltd. It explains our approach to digital accessibility, alongside the commitments above that shape how we schedule, teach and price lessons.

Our accessibility standard

We aim for our website and platform to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. This includes working towards clear colour contrast, text that can be resized without loss of function, full keyboard navigation, descriptive link and form labelling, and compatibility with common screen readers and other assistive technology.

Known limitations

We are continuously reviewing and improving accessibility across the site. Some third-party components we rely on — such as our video-calling provider used to deliver live lessons, and payment checkout screens — are operated by external providers and may not fully conform to our own accessibility standard; where this is the case, we work with those providers to seek improvements.

We have not yet commissioned a formal independent accessibility audit. We will update this statement when we have, and list any parts of the site that are known not to meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

Feedback and assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of our website or platform, or need information in a different format, please contact us at info@mpnagency.com. We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days and will do our best to provide the information, assistance or reasonable adjustment you need.

Enforcement

This statement does not affect your other legal rights. If you are unable to resolve an accessibility issue directly with us, you can seek advice from the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS) at equalityadvisoryservice.com.
