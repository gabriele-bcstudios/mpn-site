What are GCSE lessons?

GCSE lessons are designed for students working towards a GCSE in Italian or Spanish. Rather than general conversation practice, every lesson is aligned with your exam board and syllabus: building the vocabulary and grammar you are assessed on, practising the four skills — listening, speaking, reading and writing — and improving your exam technique.

Lessons will be live and online, in Italian and Spanish, with tutors who receive in-house training from experienced, accredited GCSE and A-level teachers.
