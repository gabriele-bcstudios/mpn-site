Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy explains how MPN Agency Ltd, trading as MPN — Making People Native ("MPN", "we", "us"), uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) and the UK GDPR. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. "Similar technologies", such as your browser's local storage, store small pieces of information on your device in the same way. Under PECR we need your consent to store or read information on your device, unless it is strictly necessary to provide something you have asked for.

3. What this website uses

This website does not set cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Because of that, we do not show a cookie banner. If we ever add non-essential cookies we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

4. Third-party content

  • Adobe Fonts: our fonts are loaded from Adobe's servers. This does not use cookies, but your browser connects to Adobe, which receives your IP address and technical details as part of delivering the fonts and counting use.
  • Kit (email list): if you submit your email address to join our launch list or newsletter, it is sent to Kit, our email-marketing provider. No Kit cookies are set simply by visiting our pages.

When you follow a link to another website, including our learning platform, that site may use its own cookies. The platform will have its own cookie information, including strictly necessary cookies that keep you logged in.

5. Managing storage on your device

You can view, block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing local storage means the newsletter pop-up may appear again.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if we change how we use cookies or similar technologies. The "Last updated" date shows the current version.

7. Contact us and complaints

If you have questions about this policy, email info@mpnagency.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
