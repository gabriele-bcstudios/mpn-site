About MPN

Who we are

MPN — Making People Native — is an online tutoring agency offering premium, personalised Italian and Spanish lessons. We connect learners with carefully selected native-level tutors and give them a simple platform to book, learn and keep track of their progress.

How we teach

We believe you learn a language by using it. From the very first lesson you speak, practise everyday situations and receive personalised feedback. Each lesson is shaped around your level, interests and goals.

What makes us different

  • Tutors chosen with care, through a selection process.
  • Simple, flexible booking with tokens: one token equals one lesson.
  • One platform for your lessons, materials and progress.
  • A shared learning profile, so another tutor can continue where you left off.
