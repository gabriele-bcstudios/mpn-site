What is Freestyle?

Freestyle is not a fixed course. Instead of following a set syllabus, your tutor builds each lesson around what you want to achieve: holding real conversations, preparing for a trip or a move, communicating at work, getting ready for an interview or a test, or simply enjoying the language.

You can start at any level, from Basic to Advanced, and your tutor adapts the pace, topics and exercises as you progress. Every lesson is live, online and available in both Italian and Spanish.

How Freestyle lessons work

See the full process

Tell us your goals

Choose Italian or Spanish, pick your level and tell us what you want to achieve — on your own, or with help from our team.

Meet your native tutor

You are matched with a native-speaker tutor and can choose a main tutor plus two backup tutors, so you always have an alternative.

Speak from lesson one

Lessons are live and conversation-led, one-to-one or in a small group. One token equals one lesson, so booking stays simple.

Keep your progress

Your lesson notes and materials stay saved in your personal area, and your shared learning profile follows you if you change tutor.
