Every lesson you complete earns a token, and your payout is calculated and released automatically every week — no invoices to raise. You can track your full earnings history, including upcoming payouts, from your tutor account at any time.

Our cancellation policy protects your time. Lessons cancelled inside the platform's short notice window are still credited towards your weekly earnings, so a late cancellation never costs you the lesson.

We ask every tutor to commit to a minimum weekly availability window aligned to UK time, so students can book with confidence. Beyond that minimum, you set your own hours and can adjust them week to week from your account.
