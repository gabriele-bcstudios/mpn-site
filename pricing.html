Frequently Asked Questions

One token equals one lesson, whether you book a one-to-one lesson or a group session.

When you sign up, you can choose a main tutor and two backup tutors. If your main tutor is unavailable, you can book with one of your backup tutors, subject to their availability.

Our tutors have relevant teaching experience and/or receive in-house training from experienced, accredited GCSE and A-level teachers to help them deliver effective, engaging lessons.

We select tutors based on their qualifications, experience, teaching skills and motivation, as well as their passion for sharing their language and culture. All our language tutors are native speakers of the language they teach and have sufficient English proficiency to communicate clearly with students.
