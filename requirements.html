What makes an
application stand out

Exam board experience

Hands-on experience preparing students for GCSE or A-Level exams helps you get matched with certification-track students faster, and often means a higher lesson rate.

Bilingual specialism

Tutors comfortable teaching both Italian and Spanish, or a related language, open up more weekly booking opportunities.

Business and professional language

Experience supporting working professionals with conversational or business-focused lessons is always in demand from our Freestyle students.
