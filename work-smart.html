scheduling

Shared whiteboard
& materials library

Every lesson comes with a built-in shared whiteboard, reachable with a single click — perfect for grammar breakdowns, corrections, or working through exercises together in real time.

Your own materials library lets you save worksheets, vocabulary lists, and lesson summaries once and reuse or share them with any student, so your best resources are never more than a few clicks away.

Lesson history
that travels with you

Every note, correction, and shared material is saved to a student's lesson history. If you're covering for another tutor, or a student splits their lessons across your team, you can pick up exactly where the last lesson left off.
