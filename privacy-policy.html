Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how MPN Agency Ltd, trading as MPN — Making People Native ("MPN", "we", "us", "our"), uses personal data when you use our website, book a discovery call, contact us or join our mailing list. We are the controller of that personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018.

  • Company: MPN Agency Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 17515460.
  • Registered office: Flat 11, 48 Lynton Road, London, England, W3 9HW.
  • Contact for privacy matters: info@mpnagency.com.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer because we are not required to; the contact above deals with all data protection questions. We pay, or will pay, the data protection fee to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as required, and we show our ICO registration number on our Legal Information page.

This policy covers this website. When our learning platform opens, the personal data used inside your account (lessons, payments, messages) will be described in the platform's own privacy information, which follows the same principles.

3. Children and young people

Our lessons may be taken by people under 18 with the involvement of a parent or guardian, who should make bookings and give any consent on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13 without a parent or guardian's consent, and we follow the ICO's Children's Code when designing our services. If you believe a child has given us personal data without a parent's agreement, please contact us at info@mpnagency.com and we will delete it.

4. Matching tools and automated suggestions

We may offer a questionnaire or matching tool (which may use artificial intelligence) that suggests tutors or groups based on language, level, goals, availability and, where you choose to share them, age range and group preferences. These are suggestions only: people at MPN can review them, and we do not make decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you solely by automated means. You can ask us for human review or object to this processing at any time.

5. Who we share your data with

We only share personal data with organisations that help us run the service, under written agreements and on our instructions (our "processors"), or where the law requires it. They include:

  • hosting and infrastructure providers for this website and the platform;
  • an email delivery provider (Resend) that sends booking confirmations and service emails;
  • an email-marketing provider (Kit) that holds our launch list and newsletter subscribers;
  • Google (Google Meet and Google Calendar) where a video link or calendar event is created for your call;
  • Stripe, our payment provider, which processes payments on the platform and is responsible for the card details you enter with it, and the video-lesson tools used on the platform;
  • professional advisers (lawyers, accountants, insurers) and public authorities where we are legally required to disclose information.

The website also loads fonts from Adobe Fonts, which means your browser contacts Adobe's servers and your IP address is visible to Adobe when a page loads. Tutors only see the information they need to teach you.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of the providers above are based in, or may process data in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. When this happens we make sure the transfer is protected by an appropriate safeguard: a UK adequacy decision or the UK–US Data Bridge for certified providers, or the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement / UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional measures needed. You can ask us for details at info@mpnagency.com.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted and password-protected access to our systems, security headers and input checks on our website, and written agreements with our processors. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will tell the ICO within 72 hours and, where there is a high risk to you, tell you directly.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your data (this policy);
  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data erased in certain circumstances;
  • restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format, where we use it on the basis of consent or contract;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests and, at any time, to direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent; and
  • ask for human review of automated decisions, where they apply.

To exercise a right, email info@mpnagency.com. We normally reply within one month, we do not charge a fee in most cases, and we may need to check your identity first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please contact us if you have a concern.

10. Email marketing

We only send marketing emails (such as the launch announcement or newsletters) to people who have asked to receive them. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to info@mpnagency.com to be removed. Service messages, such as a booking confirmation, are not marketing.

11. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other sites (for example Google Calendar, social media or the ICO). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version. If we make a significant change we will take reasonable steps to tell you, for example by email or a notice on the website.
