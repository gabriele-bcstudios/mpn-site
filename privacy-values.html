Privacy is a value, not a formality

You're trusting us with a lot when you book a lesson — your schedule, your goals, sometimes your exam results. We don't take that lightly. Here's how we think about your data, in plain terms.

Privacy

What privacy means to us.

These are the principles behind every product and process we build.

We collect only what we need

Enough to book your lessons, match you with the right tutor, and keep your account secure — nothing more.

We never sell your data

Your information is not a product. We share it only with the trusted providers that help us run MPN (such as email and hosting), under contract, and use it solely to run and improve your learning experience.

You stay in control

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, at any time — just email info@mpnagency.com. Your rights are explained in our Privacy Policy.

This page reflects our approach and values around privacy. For the full legal details, read our full Privacy Policy.
